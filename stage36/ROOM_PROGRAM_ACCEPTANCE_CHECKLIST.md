# Stage 36 Room Program Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] Review each room's target clear dimensions against the 54 × 38 m footprint.
- [ ] Confirm all required use types are present and assigned to the intended floor.
- [ ] Validate public, staff-secure, restricted, vehicle-secure, and high-security boundaries.
- [ ] Trace a detainee route from vehicle arrival through booking, processing, holding, and interview without crossing the public lobby.
- [ ] Trace an evidence chain from intake/logging to controlled storage without passing through public areas.
- [ ] Check dispatch sightlines, console spacing, acoustic isolation, backup power, and radio/IT cooling.
- [ ] Check garage stall count, turning radii, overhead clearances, exhaust strategy, and separated pedestrian path.
- [ ] Confirm every door clear width, door swing, access-control side, and corridor pinch point in the actual model.
- [ ] Confirm stair/elevator access and emergency egress across all occupied floors.
- [ ] Review locker/shower privacy, accessible facilities, staff support, and SWAT equipment issue workflow.
- [ ] Have qualified professionals review accessibility, fire/life safety, structure, security, and helipad requirements.
- [ ] Run the Stage 36 schedule audit and resolve errors.
- [ ] Update Blender geometry from this schedule only after design review; do not treat schedule validation as geometry validation.
- [ ] Keep the project marked not production-ready until Blender/Sollumz/CodeWalker and multiplayer FiveM acceptance passes are documented.
