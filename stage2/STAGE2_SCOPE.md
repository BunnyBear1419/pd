# Stage 2 Scope — Modern Custom Police HQ

## Blockout intent

- Modern, purpose-built civic/police headquarters.
- Three occupied floors plus a roof service/helipad deck.
- Approximate main footprint: 54 m × 38 m.
- Approximate floor-to-floor height: 4.2 m.
- Front/public entrance faces negative Y in the Blender scene.
- Separate named Blender collections so the model can be refined without flattening everything into one mesh.

## Initial functional zoning

### Ground floor
- Public lobby and reception.
- Booking/intake and temporary holding.
- Secure cell block zone.
- Vehicle garage/service access.
- Staff circulation and vertical circulation cores.

### Level 2
- Dispatch/communications.
- SWAT equipment and briefing.
- Evidence and armory secure zones.
- Staff support and circulation.

### Level 3
- Detective offices.
- Interview rooms.
- Administrative offices and meeting/support areas.

### Roof
- Marked helipad.
- HVAC/service equipment zone separated from the pad.
- Parapet and roof-access placeholders.

## Still to model in the next stage

- Proper room-by-room dimensions and wall openings.
- Door frames, door leaves, secure cell doors, and access control.
- Walkable stairs with landings and a validated elevator arrangement.
- Garage vehicle entry, turning envelope, and clear height.
- Emergency exits, accessible routes, and fire/life-safety review.
- Furniture, consoles, bunks, evidence shelving, lockers, and SWAT racks.
- Final collision geometry, material/texture sets, lighting and signage.
- Sollumz/CodeWalker export, resource manifest, and in-game placement tests.

This layout is a game-environment concept, not a code-compliant architectural or life-safety design.
