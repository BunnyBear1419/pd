# Stage 15 — Site and Perimeter Review Checklist

## Boundary and access
- [ ] Site boundary matches the chosen location and available apron.
- [ ] Main gate opening is correctly aligned to the actual driveway.
- [ ] Service-gate location is verified and has a matching fence opening if required.
- [ ] Emergency access, accessible paths, and fire/maintenance access remain clear.
- [ ] Largest intended vehicle can enter, turn, and exit without clipping.

## Security and lighting
- [ ] Fence height and construction suit the intended visual style.
- [ ] No fence posts/rails intersect walls, doors, canopy, or approach guides.
- [ ] Checkpoint/gate details are planned without blocking emergency egress.
- [ ] Lighting locations avoid window glare and excessive overlap.
- [ ] Final light sources, shadows, and performance will be tested in FiveM.

## Landscape and drainage
- [ ] Planting beds do not block sightlines or vehicle clearance.
- [ ] No landscape objects obstruct doors, accessible paths, or emergency routes.
- [ ] Drainage and grading guides are replaced by a reviewed final ground/drainage plan.
- [ ] Site ground elevation and transitions are checked in Blender and in game.

## Export readiness
- [ ] This guide collection is reviewed and hidden/removed from export if appropriate.
- [ ] Production fence, gates, lighting, landscaping, collisions, and materials are created separately.
- [ ] No claim of final optimization until the resource is exported and tested in FiveM.
