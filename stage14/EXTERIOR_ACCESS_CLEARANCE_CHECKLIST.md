# Stage 14 — Exterior Access and Clearance Checklist

## Frontage
- [ ] Public entrance path is centered on the actual entrance opening.
- [ ] Covered drop-off does not block doors or accessible circulation.
- [ ] Accessible route is continuous, unobstructed, and appropriately graded in the final model.
- [ ] Bollards protect the façade without blocking the central entry or emergency egress.

## Vehicle access
- [ ] Actual garage entrance/exit position has been confirmed; provisional guides have been moved to match.
- [ ] Service/delivery approach matches a real opening and does not cross secure circulation unexpectedly.
- [ ] Emergency lane remains clear of drop-off, parking, bollards, and exterior props.
- [ ] Largest planned vehicle can approach, turn, and depart without clipping walls or props.
- [ ] Pedestrian crossing zones are visible and do not create unsafe conflicts with vehicle paths.

## Production readiness
- [ ] Guide collection reviewed in Blender from top, frontage, and driver-eye viewpoints.
- [ ] Overlapping or misplaced guide objects corrected.
- [ ] Guide-only geometry hidden or removed from export selection.
- [ ] Final collision, lighting, materials, LODs, occlusion/portal setup, and Sollumz metadata handled in the production pipeline.
- [ ] Test in FiveM with multiple vehicle sizes and confirm no collision/streaming issues.

**Reminder:** this checklist is not an automated pass/fail certification. Stage 14 produces provisional scene guides only.
