# Stage 29 — Room dimensions and clearance acceptance checklist

## Room layout
- [ ] Verify usable interior width and depth for every required room on every floor.
- [ ] Confirm the planned room use fits its furniture and staff workflow.
- [ ] Check corridor widths at doors, corners, junctions, and furniture pinch points.
- [ ] Check stair and elevator landings and the slab openings reconciled in Stage 26.
- [ ] Check public, staff-only, and secure circulation remain appropriately separated.

## Doors and furniture
- [ ] Verify actual clear opening between jambs; do not rely on leaf/proxy width.
- [ ] Check door swing against desks, benches, cabinets, and adjacent door swings.
- [ ] Inspect booking counters, holding benches, dispatch consoles, evidence cabinets, armory storage, interview tables, lockers, and showers.
- [ ] Check wheelchair approach/turning spaces and unobstructed exits against the applicable local requirements.
- [ ] Review any reported fixture overlap manually in Blender and document accepted false positives.

## Audit and export
- [ ] Read `PDHQ_STAGE29_ROOM_CLEARANCE_REPORT`.
- [ ] Resolve or document every material clearance finding.
- [ ] Re-run Stages 17, 27, 28, and 29 after layout changes.
- [ ] Hide/exclude review guides and non-production proxies before export.
- [ ] Validate exported assets in CodeWalker and test the resource in FiveM.

## Acceptance rule
A clean automated report is not a building-code or accessibility certification. Stage 29 is accepted only after visual review of the final geometry and applicable professional/code review where required.
