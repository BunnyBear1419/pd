- [ ] Run the orchestrated Blender build and confirm the Stage 26 step succeeds.
- [ ] Inspect all three floor plates and the roof deck from above and below.
- [ ] Confirm stair and elevator openings are clear on every relevant level.
- [ ] Confirm the solid core masses are absent and only non-solid guide empties remain.
- [ ] Check tile seams, wall/slab intersections, headroom, landings, and stair continuity.
- [ ] Verify roof access and ensure the helipad/HVAC clearances remain sensible.
- [ ] Review collision separately; guide empties are not collision assets.
- [ ] Re-run Stage 16–22 audits and manually review their reports.
