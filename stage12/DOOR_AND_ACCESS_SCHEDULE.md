# Stage 12 Door and Access Schedule

| Area / group | Intended classification | Stage 12 representation | Required follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public lobby | Public | Public-finish door proxy | Verify visitor path and reception visibility |
| Staff corridors and offices | Staff-only | Staff door proxy with vision panel | Confirm opening width and clearance |
| Holding-cell / secure ground-floor areas | Restricted | Dark door proxy and illustrative reader | Replace with correct secure-room layout and actual access logic |
| Evidence and armory | Restricted | Dark door proxy and illustrative readers | Validate exact room boundary and evidence-security needs |
| Detective / interview areas | Staff-only / controlled | Staff door proxies | Verify privacy, accessibility, and circulation |
| Roof access | Restricted | Reader prop placeholder | Locate the real roof stair/door opening before export |

## Review before export
- [ ] Confirm every door leaf fits entirely inside its intended opening.
- [ ] Confirm door leaf does not intersect Stage 3's lintel or side-frame posts.
- [ ] Choose hinge side and opening direction for each door.
- [ ] Check accessibility, corridor width, and furniture clearance.
- [ ] Replace visual reader props with actual gameplay integration only after the layout is approved.
- [ ] Test collision and door behavior in a FiveM test resource.
