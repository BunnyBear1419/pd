# Wayfinding & Department Signage Plan

Stage 8 provides a replaceable starting kit for agency-neutral navigation.

## Included label groups
- **Public services:** Reception, Booking.
- **Operations:** Dispatch, Evidence, Interview Rooms, Detectives, Armory.
- **Staff and specialist areas:** Locker Rooms, SWAT Briefing, Roof Access.
- **Garage:** Secure Vehicle Bay, Authorized Vehicles Only.
- **Directories:** floor-level destination summaries.

## Rebrand checklist
1. Update the Stage 7 `BRAND` dictionary.
2. Update the Stage 8 `BRAND` dictionary to the same agency name, acronym, and palette.
3. Edit labels to suit the target organization (for example, FIB, sheriff, or private security).
4. Remove labels for departments not present in the intended server build.
5. Reposition every sign against the final geometry and verify readable text at player height.

## Placement and production caveats
Coordinates are deliberately exposed in `SIGN_LAYOUT` and are provisional blockout locations. They have not been validated against a rendered Blender scene. Room names are generic suggestions, not authoritative floor-plan confirmation. Sign backs and text are low-detail modeling proxies. Final work still requires suitable fonts/logos, UVs or decals, collision/LOD decisions, Sollumz export, and in-game checks.
