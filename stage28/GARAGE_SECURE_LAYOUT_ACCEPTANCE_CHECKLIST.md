# Stage 28 — Garage and secure ground-floor acceptance checklist

## Vehicle movement
- [ ] Confirm the final garage opening and whether entry/exit are shared or separate.
- [ ] Test the largest intended police/SWAT vehicle and a long-wheelbase van.
- [ ] Check approach, turning, reversing, bay entry, and exit swept paths.
- [ ] Confirm no door leaf, pillar, workbench, cabinet, or parked vehicle blocks the aisle.
- [ ] Keep emergency access and fire egress routes clear.

## People and secure transfer
- [ ] Identify a protected pedestrian path from staff access to the building.
- [ ] Review the route from garage to booking/holding without routing detainees through public reception.
- [ ] Confirm access-control doors and observation points in the final design.
- [ ] Check door clear widths, turning space, and circulation at corridor junctions.
- [ ] Check accessible route and local building/fire requirements with qualified review as applicable.

## Scene and export
- [ ] Read `PDHQ_STAGE28_GARAGE_SECURE_REPORT` in Blender.
- [ ] Manually inspect every reported possible obstruction and false positive.
- [ ] Adjust provisional guide coordinates to the final architecture.
- [ ] Hide/exclude `PDHQ_STAGE28_GARAGE_SECURE_REVIEW` before export.
- [ ] Re-run the scene and layout audits after approved geometry changes.
- [ ] Validate the actual exported resource in CodeWalker and FiveM.

## Acceptance rule

Stage 28 is accepted only after a human reviewer signs off on the real scene geometry and vehicle paths. A clean AABB report alone is not acceptance and does not establish production readiness.
