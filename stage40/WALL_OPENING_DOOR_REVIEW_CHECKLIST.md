# Stage 40 — Wall / opening / door review checklist

- [ ] Inspect every candidate against the actual Blender wall geometry.
- [ ] Confirm room IDs, wall thicknesses, wall intersections and structural constraints.
- [ ] Confirm opening width, door leaf, swing, threshold and accessible clear route.
- [ ] Review public, controlled, secure, high-security and vehicle-secure transitions.
- [ ] Check that corridor connections are not mistaken for direct room-to-room doors.
- [ ] Resolve every Stage 40 review item or document why it remains open.
- [ ] Re-run the generator after approved room-placement changes.
- [ ] Keep production_ready false until real geometry and external validation exist.
