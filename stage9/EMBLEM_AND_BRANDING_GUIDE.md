# Emblem and Branding Guide

## Design intent
Use a consistent agency identity across the exterior entrance and public lobby while keeping the asset editable and lightweight. The generated badge uses a simple shield silhouette, two-tone inset, metallic ring, agency initials, agency name, and optional motto.

## Placement checks
- Move the exterior emblem so it is centered on the actual entrance façade and does not overlap the Stage 7 sign or architectural trim.
- Move the lobby emblem onto a visible reception wall; confirm the face points toward the room.
- Confirm both badges clear doors, windows, ceiling elements, and furniture.
- Check that text remains legible at normal player viewing distance, especially at the smaller lobby scale.
- Test long names such as federal agencies or private security brands; reduce text size or simplify the subtitle if needed.

## Optimization and export
- Keep the emblem meshes low-detail; avoid adding dense bevels or unnecessary subdivision.
- Consider baking the final design into a small texture atlas only after the layout and branding are approved.
- Confirm material slots, normals, collision needs, LOD policy, draw distance, and resource streaming behavior in the actual Sollumz/CodeWalker/FiveM pipeline.
- The Python packaging workflow only checks syntax and expected files. It does not run Blender or certify an in-game MLO.
