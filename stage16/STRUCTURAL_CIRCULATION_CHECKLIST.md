# Stage 16 — Structural and Circulation Review Checklist

## Floor plates and structure
- [ ] Confirm all three occupied floor slabs and roof deck are at intended elevations.
- [ ] Check slab thickness, perimeter edges, and wall intersections in side/section views.
- [ ] Cut and verify stairwell openings through required slabs.
- [ ] Cut and verify aligned elevator-shaft openings through required slabs.
- [ ] Review duct, plumbing, electrical, and other planned service penetrations.
- [ ] Check for coplanar faces, z-fighting, duplicate surfaces, and hidden placeholder masses.

## Stairs and vertical circulation
- [ ] Verify each stair flight connects to the correct landing and floor.
- [ ] Confirm consistent stair direction, headroom, width, and clearance.
- [ ] Confirm stair doors/openings align with the stair enclosure.
- [ ] Ensure placeholder stair-core masses do not hide or intersect final flights.
- [ ] Inspect roof access and helipad access route.

## Doors, rooms, and accessibility
- [ ] Check partition door gaps against planned door leaf/frame dimensions.
- [ ] Confirm corridors and doorways remain clear of furniture and equipment.
- [ ] Review accessible route from public entrance to required areas.
- [ ] Review secure circulation between booking, holding, evidence, armory, and staff areas.
- [ ] Confirm no partition or furnishing blocks required egress routes.

## Audit and versioning
- [ ] Run audit_stage16_structure.py after Stages 2 and 3.
- [ ] Resolve or document each BLOCKER, WARNING, and REVIEW finding.
- [ ] Save a new version before substantial geometry changes.
- [ ] Re-run audit, then continue with manual Blender, Sollumz, CodeWalker, and FiveM validation.

Passing the script is not proof of structural correctness, accessibility compliance, or MLO readiness.
